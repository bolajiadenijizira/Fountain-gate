ACADEMICS

Learning That Builds Bright Futures.

At Fountain Gate Montessori School, we provide a balanced learning environment where children can discover their abilities, develop confidence and build a strong foundation for the future.

OUR ACADEMIC APPROACH

Education beyond the classroom.

At Fountain Gate Montessori School, we believe education should help children understand the world around them while developing their individual talents and abilities.

Our approach encourages children to ask questions, explore ideas, solve problems and participate actively in their learning.

We aim to create a welcoming environment where pupils can develop academically, socially, creatively and personally.
