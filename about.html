WHO WE ARE

Building a strong foundation for every child.

Fountain Gate Montessori School was founded in 2015 and is located in Jalingo, Taraba State, Nigeria.

Our goal is to create a safe, supportive and stimulating environment where children can develop academically, socially and creatively.
