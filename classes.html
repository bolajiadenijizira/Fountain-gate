OUR CLASSES

Learning begins with the right foundation.

At Fountain Gate Montessori School, we provide a supportive learning environment where children can develop academically, socially, creatively, and personally.

OUR LEARNING LEVELS

A place for every stage of learning.

Our classes are designed to meet children at their different stages of development. Each level provides age-appropriate learning, activities, guidance, and opportunities for children to discover their abilities.

We focus on building strong academic foundations while helping pupils develop confidence, discipline, creativity, communication skills, and good character.

01

Child-Centred Learning

We encourage pupils to participate, ask questions, explore ideas, and learn through meaningful activities.

THE LEARNING EXPERIENCE

More than just a classroom.

We believe children learn best when they are encouraged to participate, explore, create, and work together.

01

Interactive Lessons

Lessons are designed to encourage pupils to participate actively instead of simply listening and memorising.

02

Practical Activities

Pupils are given opportunities to connect classroom knowledge with practical activities and everyday experiences.

03

Creative Development

Art, creative projects, presentations, and other activities help pupils express their ideas and develop their talents.

04

Teamwork

Pupils learn to communicate, cooperate, respect others, and contribute positively to group activities.
